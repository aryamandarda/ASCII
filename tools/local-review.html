Walking mechanics

A 1.75 m adult strolling at 0.82 m/s, with a 1.36 m stride: 72 steps/min. Legs use fixed 42.9 cm thighs and 43.1 cm shins; each foot spends 60% of its cycle in stance. This is the same rig used in the street.

The face and hair share one skull frame. Turning is continuous, with no front/back switch. In the street, feet advance from measured travel distance and settle as walkers stop.

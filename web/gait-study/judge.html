Visual review rubric

  • No snap at heel strike or toe-off; no visible whole-body hop.
  • Legs carry weight; swing foot clears the ground without marching.
  • Shoulders, arms and pelvis move together; limbs do not look detached.
  • Torso and neck remain readable from the side; face profile is coherent.
  • Character detail remains consistent with the scene at both grid densities.

Reference photographs have different lighting and anatomy. They are a movement reference, not a pixel-perfect target. Automated tests do not certify that a character looks human.
